## What to build

Make AssetRun retain completed work directly in the Run Executor's authoritative RunWorkRecord throughout synchronous execution and terminal cleanup. Users retain accurate attempts, progress, and mutation evidence when later work fails or cancellation occurs, without application-side reconciliation on normal return.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AssetRun writes completed successful and unsuccessful Asset and Archive extraction outcomes into the executor-owned record before publishing attempt progress or starting another operation. Remove duplicate attempt storage and independent accumulated AssetRun results rather than retaining them for internal compatibility.
- [ ] Remove ApplicationRunWork's mirrored attempt recording, success counters, and end-of-call record replacement. Operation adapters return outcomes; the work module owns recording and phase-local completed/succeeded/failed accounting.
- [ ] Mixed successes and failures advance completed progress exactly once per attempt; totals, phase applicability, ordering, and cancellation checkpoints remain compatible.
- [ ] Preserve conversion of operation-adapter exceptions into unsafe outcomes with uncertain mutation evidence. Retain cancellation independently of unsafe continuation, including cancellation during the final atomic attempt, and prevent subsequent work or Archive Finalization under existing rules.
- [ ] Retain definitive Routing Ledger state, Archive Collisions, recognized exclusions, and cancellation without normal-return reconciliation. Interrupted discovery remains distinguishable from a definitive empty Routing Ledger; earlier extraction evidence survives.
- [ ] Record each returned Archive Finalization aggregate once, retaining its output attempts and phase evidence and preserving finalization progress. Do not require streaming individual output attempts before the aggregate returns.
- [ ] Keep Preparing and work diagnostics and failures intact while changing record ownership, coordinating existing sink retention so evidence is not duplicated. The sibling diagnostic-publication ticket completes centralized publication and removes its remaining application cursors.
- [ ] Through production recording beneath the Run Executor, verify exception survival, mixed progress, cancellation and unsafe continuation, empty work, preflight failure and collision behavior, Dry Run non-mutation, finalization aggregates, mandatory cleanup, terminal precedence, and terminal result ownership after dependency destruction.
- [ ] Validate the changed behavior through the project's profile-triplet workflow. If blocked, diagnose and report the blocker; alternate-generator checks do not replace that validation.
- [ ] Document non-obvious ordering and lifetime constraints and added or substantially rewritten methods. Preserve accurate existing comments and update the knowledge graph after implementation changes.
- [ ] Preserve ADR-0001 and public Run Request, Run Handle, result, observation, scheduling, and lifetime contracts. Keep terminal classification and mandatory Safety Cleanup with the Run Executor.
